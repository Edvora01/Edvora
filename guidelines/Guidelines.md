# Edvora 2.0 design language

Edvora uses a precise Swiss-inspired education-tech system with an energetic two-tone canvas. Pages pair quiet slate grounds and white study surfaces with royal blue navigation, orange actions, violet competitive learning, emerald practice, and cyan digital tools.

- **Display:** Outfit, 600–800. **Body:** Manrope, 400–700. **Data/code:** DM Mono.
- Use a strict 12-column desktop grid, asymmetric feature compositions, thin blue-gray rules, 14–24px radii, and restrained elevation.
- Prefer clear labels, numbered steps, connected diagrams, and resource dashboards over repeated identical cards.
- Royal blue is the primary interaction color. Orange is reserved for high-intent actions. Never use invented social proof or statistics.
- Dark navy product canvases are reserved for Infinity Slate. JEE uses blue/violet; NEET uses emerald/cyan.
- Motion should be brief and functional: hover lift, focus rings, tab transitions, and mobile drawer movement.
- All interactive elements require visible focus states and descriptive labels. Mobile layouts collapse at roughly 1000px without losing hierarchy.
